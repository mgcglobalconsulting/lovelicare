**Client:** LoveLi Care Med Spa & Wellness Lounge
**Provider:** Libra Robertson, NP-CRNP (Board Certified Nurse Practitioner)
**Location:** 100 East Pennsylvania Ave, Suite 304, Towson, MD 21286
**Existing tagline:** Biophilic · Clinical · Luxury
**Aesthetic source:** "Tropical House | Deep House | Chill House ~ Luxury Villa Pool Party & Sunset Beach Music" — Echo Lounge Vibes

---

## 1. Why this aesthetic fits (and why it isn't arbitrary)

The brand already declares itself **Biophilic · Clinical · Luxury**. The ambient-luxury-villa visual genre is the most precise existing visual language for exactly that triad:

| Brand pillar | Villa/sunset genre equivalent |
|---|---|
| **Biophilic** | Backlit palm and monstera foliage, living shade, water, warm stone, raw linen |
| **Clinical** | Architectural restraint — flat planes, infinity-edge geometry, nothing cluttered, surgical negative space |
| **Luxury** | Golden-hour light, deep water, brass and travertine, unhurried pacing, privacy |

The genre is not "beach vacation." It is **controlled sensory calm at a high price point** — which is the exact emotional product a med spa sells. A client books Botox or an IV drip to buy back composure. The visual system should feel like the state they are purchasing.

**Strategic read:** LoveLi Care is a clinical practice that competes on *feeling*, not on price or equipment lists. Towson has other injectors. It does not have a practice that feels like a private villa.

## 2. The single most important guardrail

> **Translate atmosphere, never iconography.**

"Tropical" in this system means **light quality, humidity, shade, water, and warm stone**. It never means tropical *objects*.

### Permanently banned
- Pineapples, hibiscus, monstera *as a printed pattern*, flamingos, parrots, toucans
- Tiki anything — torches, masks, bamboo-pole framing, thatch
- Surfboards, shells, starfish, sand dollars, nautical rope, anchors
- Script fonts, "Aloha / Paradise / Oasis / Escape" headline copy
- Cocktail-with-umbrella imagery, coconut cups
- Saturated turquoise + hot pink "beach club" palettes
- Sunset *gradients as decoration* (gradient buttons, gradient text, gradient blobs)

### Always permitted
- The **shadow** a palm frond casts across plaster
- **Caustics** — the rippling light pattern water throws on a surface
- **Haze** and atmospheric depth, soft bloom around light sources
- **Travertine, limewash, teak, unglazed ceramic, raw linen, brushed brass**
- **Horizon lines** and wide, calm framing
- **Golden-hour directional light** as the lighting model for all photography
- A **single** live plant in frame, real and specific — not styled abundance

If a design element would look at home in a Corona commercial, it is wrong. If it would look at home in an Aman resort brochure or a Rick Owens interior, it is right.

## 3. Brand personality

| Dimension | Position | Not |
|---|---|---|
| Warmth | Genuinely warm, personal — Libra's name and face are the brand | Clinical coldness, stock-photo anonymity |
| Authority | Quiet credential-led confidence (NP-CRNP, board certified, HIPAA) | Hype, "transformation" shouting, before/after spam as the hero |
| Pace | Unhurried. Nothing rushes the user. No countdown timers | Urgency marketing, pop-ups, "only 3 spots left" |
| Luxury register | Restrained, material, private | Gold-foil maximalism, diamond icons, "VIP" badging |
| Honesty | "No pressure — just personalized guidance" (their own words) | Pressure funnels, hidden pricing, fake scarcity |

### Voice principles

1. **Short declaratives.** "Results look natural, never overdone." The existing site copy already does this well — preserve it.
2. **Name the mechanism.** Luxury wellness earns trust by explaining *why* something works. The knowledge base already does this (biotin → keratin → nail strength). Keep that specificity; it is a differentiator.
3. **Never promise outcomes.** Medical practice. "Individual results may vary" is not just legal boilerplate — write copy that doesn't need rescuing by a disclaimer.
4. **Second person, present tense.** "You arrive ten minutes early. You drink water." Calm instruction.
5. **No exclamation marks.** Anywhere.

### Words to use
nurture, restore, elevate, balance, refine, sculpt, optimize, replenish, considered, unhurried, clinical, luminous, composed

### Words to avoid
snatched *(except in the trademarked `Snatch Protocol™`)*, slay, obsessed, bestie, girlie, miracle, magic, secret, hack, anti-aging, flawless, perfect

## 4. Service architecture + emotional register

The existing site already groups services under verbs. Keep this — it is strong. Each gets its own atmospheric register within the one system:

> Palette note: this file's color references are superseded by the Vagaro **"Bare"** monochrome override. See `BRANDING.md` § 1. Material names below (travertine, linen, teak) refer to *physical materials for photography*, not color tokens — they remain correct.

| Category | Verb | Services | Atmospheric register |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Refine** | Medical Aesthetics | Botox, Fillers, HydraFacial, Microneedling, Peels | Brightest. Bone ground, high-key morning light, mirror-clean |
| **Sculpt** | Body Contouring / `Snatch Protocol™` | 360° non-invasive system | Most architectural. Deep shadow, strong directional light, form and edge |
| **Optimize** | Weight Management | Vitamin injections, diet modification | Clear, daylit, organized. Charts and structure permitted here |
| **Restore** | IV & Nutritional Wellness | Build Your Glow, Fat Burn & Energy, Immunity Fortress | Deepest, most immersive. Dusk water, lantern light, reclined comfort |
| **Nourish** | Nutrition Counseling | Assessment, meal planning | Warmest, most domestic. Teak table, ceramic, real food |
| **Concierge Medical** | Non-Emergent Care | MMCC, physicals, refills, notes | Most restrained. Nearly neutral — efficiency signals respect for time |

**Note on `Snatch Protocol™`:** This is the one place the brand's voice gets to be bold, because it is a trademarked proprietary name with equity. Treat it as a product mark — set in display face, always with ™, never explained apologetically. It is the brand's most ownable asset after Libra herself.

## 5. Trust architecture

These must be visible but never loud. They are the "clinical" leg of the tripod and the reason premium pricing holds.

- 5.0 Google Stars
- NP-CRNP Board Certified
- HIPAA Compliant
- Maryland Licensed
- Medical Director Oversight
- Complimentary clinical consultation before any treatment

**Placement rule:** a single quiet row, letterspaced small caps, hairline dividers between items, no icons, no badge graphics, no color. Credential typography should look like the bottom of a gallery wall label — not like a trust-badge plugin.

## 7. What to fix first (audit findings)

Found while reading the current build:

1. **Typography is fragmented across 10 families** — Bodoni Moda, Marcellus, Cormorant Garamond, Italiana, Playfair Display, Inter, Jost, Mulish, Montserrat, Open Sans all appear across `public/*.html`. This is the biggest single credibility leak. Consolidate to the three defined in `DESIGN-SYSTEM.md` § 2.
2. **Color values are hardcoded** — 40+ distinct hex literals, many near-duplicates (`#C9A96E` / `#D4B87A` / `#D9BE86` / `#d5ad71` / `#e0bd84` are five versions of the same gold). Adopt `tokens/tokens.css` and replace literals with custom properties.
3. **`liquid-glass.css` already exists** — good instinct, and it maps perfectly to the "pool surface" elevation model. Formalize it against the tokens rather than replacing it.
4. **Only 3 brand images exist** in `assets/img/`. The photography gap is the main blocker to the aesthetic landing. See `prompts/IMAGE-GENERATION-PROMPTS.md`.
